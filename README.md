# 🛡️ Attack on Titan (進撃の巨人)

**Author:** Hajime Isayama  
**Genres:** Action, Dark Fantasy, Post-Apocalyptic  
**Original Run:** September 9, 2009 – April 9, 2021  
**Volumes:** 34  
**Anime Adaptation:** 2013–2023  
**Studio:** Wit Studio (Seasons 1–3), MAPPA (Season 4)  
**Total Episodes:** 94 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_on_Titan_%28TV_series%29))

## 📖 Plot Summary

In a world besieged by colossal humanoid creatures known as Titans, humanity resides within massive walled cities to ensure survival. The story follows **Eren Yeager**, whose life is irrevocably changed when a Titan breaches his hometown's wall, leading to the tragic loss of his mother. Fueled by vengeance, Eren enlists in the military alongside his adoptive sister **Mikasa Ackerman** and friend **Armin Arlert**. As they ascend through the ranks, they uncover dark secrets about the Titans' origins and the true nature of their world.

## 🎬 Anime Overview

The anime adaptation closely mirrors the manga's narrative, with the final season concluding in 2023. The series is available for streaming on platforms like Crunchyroll and Funimation.

## 🧠 Themes & Impact

- **Freedom vs. Oppression:** Explores the human desire for freedom against forces that seek to oppress.  
- **Survival:** Centers on humanity's struggle for survival in a hostile world.  
- **Identity & Memory:** Delves into the complexities of identity and the impact of memory.  
- **Political Intrigue:** Incorporates elements of political maneuvering and betrayal.

*Attack on Titan* has garnered critical acclaim for its intricate storytelling, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes.
